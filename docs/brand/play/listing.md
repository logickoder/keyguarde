## Full description (1623/4000)

```
Never miss an invoice, an interview or your own name in a busy group chat again.

Keyguarde watches notifications from the chat apps you pick, looks for the words you choose, and keeps every message that has one. Everything else stays quiet.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick your words: a name, "invoice", "interview", "rent". Whole words, any case.
• Pick your apps: WhatsApp, Telegram, Messages, Signal or any other app that sends notifications.
• Matches collect in one list, with the word highlighted. Tap one to read it in full or open the chat app.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
• Get a pop-up alert when a word matches, or keep matches silent and check them later.
• Search your matches, and filter them by app or by keyword.
• See when each keyword last matched, so you know which ones still work.
• Run a live test from Settings to check Keyguarde is listening.
• Pause Keyguarde any time, without turning off notification access.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Messages, matches and keywords stay on your phone. No server, no account.
• Matches are left out of Android backups.
• Keyguarde only sees notifications as they arrive. It can't open your chats, read older messages or see media.
• The app uses Google Firebase for usage stats, crash reports and performance data, and shows Google AdMob ads. The privacy policy lists exactly what each one receives.

WHO IT'S FOR
• Job seekers watching recruitment groups
• Traders in fast signal groups
• Anyone in a group chat that's too loud to read

Keyguarde needs notification access to work. Setup asks for it and explains why.

Privacy policy: https://logickoder.dev/keyguarde/#/privacy-policy
```
